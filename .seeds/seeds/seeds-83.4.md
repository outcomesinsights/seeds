Key finding from the ConPort analysis: ConPort is fundamentally a memory bank — it stores what was decided. Seeds is fundamentally a thinking tool — it tracks the process of deciding. Seeds has unique strengths ConPort lacks: deliberation lifecycle (captured→exploring→resolved), first-class questions with open/answered status, blocking logic (parent can't resolve until children do), minimal-friction capture (jot), and the 'capture during investigation' philosophy. Should NOT adopt ConPort wholesale or replace seeds with it. Cherry-pick specific capabilities (MCP, FTS, typed links) while preserving the deliberation-first identity.
