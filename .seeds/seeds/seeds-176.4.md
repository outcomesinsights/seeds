## The stance (@aguynamedryan, 2026-06-18)
The honest frontier of the journey post. With the full source material and a comprehensive seed corpus, you *could* run an entire analytics / maintenance program over your own deliberation. @aguynamedryan, in his own words: "Pointing out the potential has some merit — it might attract people looking for at least a platform upon which to develop pipelines like that. They're not particularly interesting to me, but they are available."

**What is in the program (and why each is only possible because the journey was kept):**
- Drift audits — resolutions whose premises aged out; contradictions among resolved seeds (seeds-158/159).
- Feed implementation learnings back into the originating deliberation (seeds-161, seeds-177).
- Metrics on the deliberation itself — first-hunch hit rate, AI-suggestion hit rate, revisit frequency (seeds-160/174).
- Sharpen the pipeline by examining how it functions (seeds-174).
- Re-ingest source material as AI / understanding improves (seeds-131).

**The honest admission to keep in the post:** "I don't have quantitative data about any of this, nor qualitative data, because I'm not doing it." Even the outcome-tracking flavor, @aguynamedryan has flagged, risks becoming "a heavy chore with questionable value" (seeds-160). Frame as *available, not lived* — a horizon mostly not yet walked — not as achievement.

**Why include it at all:** (1) the potential has standalone merit and may attract platform-minded builders; (2) more importantly, it is the argument *for keeping the journey even when you do little with it* — the doing is optional, the keeping is cheap.
