Source: intent.build ("The system of record for human decisions in software"; see seeds/docs/intent-build-overview.md). Three surfaces — Capture (auto-extracts decisions from AI coding sessions; bidirectional provenance, any line <-> the session that wrote it), Arena (real-time collaboration that captures architecture-review deliberation and extracts the decisions from it — genuinely upstream), Repo (decisions versioned in .intent/). Its stated philosophy targets the same ground seeds claims: "the most important thing to preserve is human intent"; "depth of thought, not just speed"; "mindful making."

RYAN'S HONEST READ: we didn't pull in enough about intent.build, because it does seem to EAT AND DISPLACE seeds — it looks to eat a lot of seeds' lunch. Don't pretend otherwise in the post; name the threat plainly.

RYAN'S PUSHBACK (admitted: maybe partly a rationalization to keep going with seeds, but real):
1. SEEDS MEETS ME WHERE I AM. @aguynamedryan lives inside a conversation with an AI via the Claude Code CLI; seeds is right there in that loop. The AI knows how to use seeds and uses it to assist its OWN future-planning conversation with @aguynamedryan. Ingesting a transcript is trivial — pulling in human-to-human (e.g. @aguynamedryan <-> @markdanese) transcripts to supplement what's already in seeds is not a big ask. seeds is native to @aguynamedryan's actual workflow; intent.build would be a separate surface to live in.
2. DECISION/INTENT DERIVATION IS A "PARLOR TRICK." intent.build's whole focus — deriving decisions/intent from the captured material — is something @aguynamedryan is confident seeds can generate as an artifact quite easily and readily from what it already holds. He's happy to let other people chase the decision-capture question, because the decision artifact is the EASY, downstream output, not the hard part. The hard, valuable part is the journey (the deliberation about what and why), which is what seeds is built around. intent.build optimizes the byproduct; seeds optimizes the source.

CORRECTION TO PRIOR FRAMING (@aguynamedryan, verbatim-ish): "I never said seeds is upstream of EVERYTHING else — that was your [the assistant's] assertion." The claim is "seeds is upstream of INTENT" (see seeds-168), NOT "upstream of everyone/everything." Do NOT totalize ("everyone is downstream," "seeds is the only one that keeps the journey"). intent.build itself reaches upstream (Arena); the real distinction is not lifecycle position but what a tool makes FIRST-CLASS — intent.build foregrounds the decision; seeds foregrounds the deliberation/journey.

USE IN POST: give intent.build real weight (@aguynamedryan: we under-covered it). Lead with the honest threat ("it eats a lot of seeds' lunch"), then the grounded differentiation (meets-me-where-I-am + decision-derivation-is-a-parlor-trick), framed as reconciliation and honest positioning, not a takedown.
