Practitioners are using multi-agent critique patterns — e.g., ask Claude to create a plan, then have Gemini and Codex critique it. The critiques and responses are deliberation artifacts with no formal home. Seeds could capture these as first-class objects: questions from different sources, competing perspectives on the same decision, structured disagreement tracking.
