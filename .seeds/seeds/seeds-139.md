Initial thought: should seeds have a 'lodestone' concept — seeds that represent the guiding vision or north-star for a project, pinned above the noise?

Immediate self-answer: No. This is the purview of Claude's memory system (or similar persistent context like CLAUDE.md). Seeds captures the *journey* of deliberation, not the persistent identity of a project. A lodestone is a conclusion, not a deliberation — it belongs in a system designed for persistent recall, not one designed for growing and resolving ideas.
