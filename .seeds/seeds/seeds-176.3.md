## The spine (@aguynamedryan, 2026-06-18)
The post's organizing move: **destination-thinking vs. journey-thinking.** Every tool in the AI-dev conversation optimizes around a *destination* — intent-debt artifacts around the settled decision, Poll around the concrete change at review time, DeltaDB around the code as it is written. seeds is the only one sitting with the *journey*.

**Why this dissolves the could-do / actually-doing tension (seeds-176):** every item on the could-do list (audits, drift, metrics, re-ingestion, feedback loops) is something you can *only* do because you kept the journey. So "I'm barely doing those things" stops being a weakness and becomes the point: **keeping the journey is the cheap part; doing something with it is optional and arrives later** — when the AI is better, when the insight finally sparks, when the half-baked idea unlocks. You keep the deliberation precisely because you cannot predict, from the trailhead, which slice you will be grateful you remembered.

**The gap it sits over:** the whole conversation presupposes the distillation — "here is your intent / spec / decision, now record and enforce it." They start at the destination. The most valuable and most lossy part — discovering the approach to begin with — is upstream of all of it. (Relates to seeds-168, the upstream-of-intent positioning.)
