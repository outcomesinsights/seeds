The Dec 2025–Jun 2026 deliberation-software sweep (docs/deliberation-software-revival-2026-06.md) found a real surge, but the term "deliberation software" now most loudly means multi-LLM debate-and-vote COUNCILS, not human-reasoning capture. Seeded by Karpathy's llm-council (Dec 17 2025): deliberation-mcp (51 releases), council-of-high-intelligence (~968 stars), claude-synod-debate, deliberum, llm-deliberate — the agent-debate cluster outnumbers human/civic deliberation tools ~15:4 on GitHub's 'deliberation' topic.

These emit an AI-synthesized Verdict — destination-oriented, machine-reasoning — the opposite of seeds' capture-the-human-journey bet.

The hazard is positioning / discoverability, NOT product: someone searching "deliberation tool" or "deliberation MCP" in mid-2026 lands on agent-councils, not journey-capture. Sharper still, llm-deliberate markets itself as studying "the deliberation process itself rather than final outputs" — seeds' own journey-not-destination language, pointed at machine consensus. The vocabulary is being claimed.

Open question: how does seeds name / position / differentiate so it is found for what it is? Likely lean on "deliberation CAPTURE" and the upstream-of-intent framing (seeds-168) rather than the bare, now-crowded "deliberation" label.
