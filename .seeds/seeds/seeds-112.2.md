Even if an agent knows it should capture a question, there's friction: pause the conversational flow, formulate the question text, run 'seeds ask', then resume the original thread. Agents optimize for conversational momentum and treat the capture step as optional. This might be addressable by making capture cheaper (shorter syntax, less context-switching) or by making it part of the natural flow rather than an interruption.
