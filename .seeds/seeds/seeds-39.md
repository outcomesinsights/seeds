**Minimal conventions (v1):**

- `**Q:**` / `**A:**` - Questions and answers
- `**Options:**` - Alternatives being considered
- `**Decision:**` / `**Rationale:**` - Conclusions reached
- `**Considerations:**` - Thinking-through, pros/cons

**Intentionally minimal.** We're defining only the obvious patterns now. Additional conventions may emerge organically through use - we'll revisit and codify those once patterns become clear rather than speculating upfront.
