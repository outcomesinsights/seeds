Agent conversations compress into summaries that erase original reasoning chains — you cannot audit the reasoning that produced a decision. Seeds could store distilled reasoning from agent sessions as first-class artifacts, preserving the 'why' that gets lost when conversation context is compacted. This addresses the gap between full conversation transcripts (too verbose) and compressed summaries (too lossy).
