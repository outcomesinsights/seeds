Yes, but in DISTILLED form, by design. The seeds-to-beads skill splits the two cleanly: "Beads represent work; seeds carry the deliberation." The bead gets a "## Why" paragraph plus pre-written verbatim content ("so the executing agent doesn't have to re-design") and a "Source:" pointer back to the originating seeds. The full journey — discarded alternatives, the back-and-forth, why a decision changed — stays in the seed. So the intent that reaches the bead is the actionable distillation, not the whole deliberation; the journey is reachable via the citation but is not inlined. The real risk: anything the distillation step drops becomes invisible downstream.
